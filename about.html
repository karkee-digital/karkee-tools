Who We Are

KARKEE TOOLS is a free online tools platform developed by Karkee Digital Studio.

We bring useful digital tools together in one place to help people complete everyday tasks more easily. Our platform is designed with simplicity, accessibility and ease of use in mind.

What We Offer

KARKEE TOOLS brings together different categories of online utilities:

  • PDF Tools – Useful PDF-related utilities.
  • Image Tools – Tools for common image tasks.
  • Text Tools – Text editing and productivity utilities.
  • Design Tools – Helpful creative utilities.
  • Developer Tools – Utilities for developers and programmers.
  • QR & Barcode – QR code and barcode utilities.
  • Calculators – Calculations for everyday needs.
  • Converters – Tools for converting supported formats and values.

We plan to introduce more useful tools and features as the platform grows.

Our Mission
