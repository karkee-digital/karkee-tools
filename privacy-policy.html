Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Welcome to KARKEE TOOLS, a project of Karkee Digital Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used and what choices are available when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only the information needed to operate, maintain and improve our website. Depending on how you use the website, this may include:

  • Contact information you submit, such as your name and email address.
  • Messages and enquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • Website usage and visit information.
  • Technical information such as device type, browser and operating system.

2. How Tools Process Your Data

Data processing depends on the individual tool. Some tools may process content directly in your browser, while others may use external services or servers. Check the relevant tool's instructions before submitting sensitive information.

3. Website Analytics

We collect basic website visit information to understand how KARKEE TOOLS is used and improve our services. Analytics information may include:

  • Page path visited and the tool page used.
  • Date and time of the visit.
  • A randomly generated pseudonymous browser identifier stored in local storage.
  • Approximate device type, such as desktop, mobile or tablet.
  • Browser name, such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari.
  • Operating system, such as Windows, Android, iOS or macOS.

Device, browser and operating-system information is estimated from browser information and may not always be accurate.

The browser identifier is pseudonymous and is not designed to identify you by name or email address. It helps us distinguish browser visits for basic analytics. Clearing your browser storage may create a new identifier.

Analytics may include repeat visits, automated traffic or incomplete technical information. Therefore, analytics figures are estimates and are not verified counts of individual people.

We do not intentionally collect IP addresses through our website analytics tracking feature.

4. Cookies and Browser Storage

Our website and third-party services may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics and preferences.

The pseudonymous analytics identifier described above is stored in your browser's local storage. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. Doing so may cause a new identifier to be generated on a later visit.

5. Contact Form Information

If you submit our contact form, we collect the name, email address, subject and message you provide. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and manage support requests.

Contact submissions are stored using Supabase and may generate an email notification through our email service provider.

Our contact form also uses Cloudflare Turnstile for security verification and abuse prevention. The service may process technical information as described in its own privacy documentation.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to operate and maintain KARKEE TOOLS, including:

  • Supabase: Backend database and application services.
  • Cloudflare: Website hosting, delivery and security services.
  • Resend: Contact email notifications.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Contact form security verification.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website. Information may be retained for as long as necessary to operate our services, respond to enquiries, maintain security and meet applicable obligations.

No online service or method of data transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You can clear your browser's local storage to remove the analytics identifier stored on that browser. A new identifier may be generated on your next visit.

You can also choose not to submit the contact form and avoid entering sensitive information into tools unless you understand how that tool processes your data.

9. Children's Privacy

KARKEE TOOLS is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by applicable law.

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, technologies or services change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please visit our Contact page.
